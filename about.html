About

Local electrical & solar work, done right.

KPF Electrical & Renewables covers both trades under one roof — so wiring and solar decisions are made together, not patched together afterwards.

The short version

One electrician, two specialities.

Most electrical companies bolt solar on as an afterthought, and most solar companies subcontract the wiring. KPF Electrical & Renewables was set up to close that gap — every job, big or small, is handled by people who understand both sides of the meter.

That means a rewire is planned with future solar in mind, and a solar install is wired properly the first time, tested and signed off to the same standard as any other electrical job.
