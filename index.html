About KPF Electrical

One electrician, two specialities.

KPF Electrical & Renewables covers electrical, solar and data & comms under one roof — so wiring, solar and cabling decisions are made together, not patched together afterwards.

KPF Electrical & Renewables is led by Keith Flanagan, an electrician with over 20 years' experience in the trade and five years installing solar PV systems. Most electrical companies bolt solar on as an afterthought, and most solar companies subcontract the wiring — KPF Electrical & Renewables was set up to close that gap, every job handled by someone who understands both sides of the meter.

That means a rewire is planned with future solar in mind, and a solar install is wired properly the first time, tested and signed off to the same standard as any other electrical job.
